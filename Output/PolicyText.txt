Nerriza Transport Services - Lipa City
Billing Terms and Payment Policy

1. Billing: Clients are billed per parcel delivered at the agreed rate.
2. VAT: A 12% Value Added Tax is added to every invoice.
3. Payment terms: Payment is due on the number of days stated on the invoice,
counted from the invoice date.
4. Partial payments: Partial payments are accepted. The remaining balance stays
due on the original due date.
5. Overdue accounts: Invoices unpaid after the due date are overdue. A reminder
is sent, and further follow-ups may be sent until the balance is settled.
6. Payment methods: Bank transfer or check payable to Nerriza Transport Services.
7. Questions: Reply to this email or contact the Finance and Accounting office.
